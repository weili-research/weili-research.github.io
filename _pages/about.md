<div class="lab-section lab-profile"><p class="lab-kicker">PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR</p><h2>Prof. Wei Li</h2><p>Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications, China, and adjunct professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and the State Key Laboratory of Flexible Electronics.</p><p>Prof. Li received his B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Michigan State University, respectively. He completed a three-year postdoctoral fellowship in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences and the Berkeley Sensor &amp; Actuator Center at the University of California, Berkeley, and also served as an affiliate postdoctoral fellow at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.</p><p>His research interests include robotics, flexible electronics, MEMS, wireless biomedical systems, and self-powered sensing. He is an IEEE Senior Member and a National High-Level Young Talent, Distinguished Professor of Jiangsu Province, and Specially Appointed Expert of the Federation of Jiangsu Returned Overseas Chinese.</p><p><a href="mailto:wei.li@njupt.edu.cn">wei.li@njupt.edu.cn</a> · <a href="https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1396-9692">ORCID</a> · <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kiF6OS4AAAAJ&amp;hl=en">Google Scholar</a></p></div>
